Monash vs RMIT: weighing the IT degree options

A side-by-side look at strengths and weaknesses across four paths, ordered by what matters most to you.

1Course content
2Location & lifestyle
3Accommodation comfort
4Overall cost
Monash · Clayton

Bachelor of IT

Single degree, 3 years

Strengths

  • Accommodation is a one-minute walk to class — no early starts
  • Large, natural campus with a calmer, greener feel
  • Cheaper accommodation than RMIT's baseline rate
  • Keeps career direction flexible if you're still deciding

Weaknesses

  • Twin beds only in accommodation
  • Further from the city — less immediately "on your doorstep"
  • No business content built into the degree
  • No equivalent to the Apple Foundation Program
Monash · Clayton

Bachelor of IT + Digital Business

Double degree, longer duration

Strengths

  • Substantial, multi-year business content — matches your actual interest, not just a bonus extra
  • Same one-minute walk to class as the single degree
  • Broadens career options across tech and business
  • Large, natural campus feel

Weaknesses

  • Longer degree — more time and cost overall
  • Twin beds only
  • Further from the city
  • No Apple Foundation Program
RMIT · Melbourne City

Bachelor of IT

Regular stream · 3 years · ATAR ~70

Strengths

  • City campus, close to things to do
  • Queen bed accommodation option available
  • Can add the Apple Foundation Program — free, 4 weeks, low-stress add-on
  • Finishes in 3 years, lower ATAR requirement
  • Possible accommodation discount if the financial aid voucher applies

Weaknesses

  • No structured industry internship built in
  • More expensive accommodation baseline than Monash
  • No dedicated business/double-degree content stream
RMIT · Melbourne City

Bachelor of IT (Professional)

4 years · ATAR ~83–84 · includes internship year

Strengths

  • Built-in paid industry internship in the final year
  • Capstone project and programming studios for real-world experience
  • RMIT organises the internship placement for you
  • City campus, queen bed option, Apple Foundation Program available
  • Strong professional network before you graduate

Weaknesses

  • 4 years total — one more year than the other three options
  • Higher entry score — confirm your ATAR clears ~83–84
  • Same accommodation cost consideration as the regular RMIT stream
Ranked by your stated priorities: course content weighs heaviest, so the real fork is between Monash's Digital Business double degree and RMIT's Professional stream — both meaningfully deepen your degree, just in different directions.
